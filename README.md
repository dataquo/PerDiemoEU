## 🚀 Overview

PerDiemo eliminates manual spreadsheet calculations for domestic and foreign business travel (*pracovní cesty*). Designed for corporate finance teams, accountants, and business travelers, PerDiemo provides statutory compliance with:
* **Czech Labour Code (*Zákoník práce*) §§ 163 & 170**: Exact domestic and foreign allowance brackets.
* **Statutory Meal Reductions (*Krácení stravného*)**: Automated deductions for complimentary breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
* **Decisive Currency Fixing (*§ 183 Zákoníku práce*)**: Automated synchronization with daily Czech National Bank (ČNB) fixing rates for foreign expenses.
* **AI Receipt & Ticket OCR**: Extraction of travel routes, hotel stays, board provisions, and departure/arrival timestamps.
